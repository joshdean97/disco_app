Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Disco collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use the Disco platform.

By using Disco, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Who We Are

Disco is a hospitality staffing marketplace connecting hospitality operators and flexible workers.

For privacy-related questions, you can contact:

support@yourdiscodomain.com

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information when you use Disco:

Account Information

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Username
  • Password (stored securely and encrypted)
  • Account type (worker or operator)

Profile Information

  • Work experience
  • Skills and roles
  • Availability information
  • Profile descriptions
  • Venue information

Platform Activity

  • Shift applications
  • Accepted shifts
  • Messages and interactions
  • Reliability metrics and activity data

Technical Information

  • IP address
  • Browser type
  • Device information
  • Pages visited
  • Login activity

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide and operate the Disco platform
  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Match workers and operators
  • Display shift opportunities
  • Improve platform performance and user experience
  • Monitor platform security and prevent abuse
  • Communicate with users regarding their accounts
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. Legal Basis for Processing

Under UK GDPR, we process personal information based on:

  • User consent
  • Performance of a contract
  • Legitimate business interests
  • Legal obligations

5. Sharing Your Information

Disco may share limited information between workers and operators to facilitate shift applications and bookings.

For example:

  • Workers may see venue details
  • Operators may see worker profile information
  • Operators may view availability and application history

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

6. Third-Party Services

Disco may use trusted third-party providers for services such as:

  • Hosting and infrastructure
  • Analytics
  • Email delivery
  • Authentication and security

These providers may process information on our behalf under appropriate security measures.

7. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect user data from unauthorized access, misuse, loss, or disclosure.

However, no online platform can guarantee absolute security.

8. Data Retention

We retain information only as long as reasonably necessary to:

  • Provide platform services
  • Maintain business records
  • Resolve disputes
  • Comply with legal obligations

Users may request deletion of their accounts subject to applicable legal requirements.

9. Your Rights

Under UK data protection laws, users may have the right to:

  • Access their personal data
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of personal data
  • Restrict or object to processing
  • Request transfer of their data
  • Withdraw consent where applicable

Requests can be made by contacting:

support@yourdiscodomain.com

10. Cookies and Analytics

Disco may use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Keep users logged in
  • Improve platform functionality
  • Analyze traffic and usage patterns
  • Enhance security

Users may manage cookie settings through their browser.

11. Children’s Privacy

Disco is not intended for users under the age of 18.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

12. International Data Transfers

Some service providers used by Disco may store or process data outside the United Kingdom.

Where this occurs, appropriate safeguards are used where required by law.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically.

Continued use of Disco after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

14. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact:

support@yourdiscodomain.com
